# PRODUCT · Milano Evolution

register: product

## Users
Nour and people like her: international students and foreigners who just arrived in Milan, speak English (or Chinese, Spanish, Arabic), little or no Italian, holding a phone on the street or at a counter, tired, a bit anxious, in a hurry. Secondary: the City of Milan (Comune, YesMilano) that would adopt the app.

## Product purpose
Nour never opens a portal. The app understands what she needs («I need to rent a room»), reads the City's official guides for her, gives her the steps in order and fills in the forms. Voice first at the start, then a normal touch app with optional audio.

## Brand and tone
Warm, calm, competent, local. Feels like a friendly Milanese who knows the system. Plain English with the Italian words she will hear at the counter. Never bureaucratic, never cute.

## Visual direction
Airbnb's onboarding wizard language (see DESIGN.md): white canvas, ink text, one big question per screen, soft rounded cards, one shadow tier, segmented progress, Back / Next. Milano colours replace Airbnb's Rausch: red Comune `#A50D26` for primary actions and the voice orb, yellow YesMilano `#FFC800` for highlights and progress. Isometric illustrations.

## Anti-references
- Government portals: dense pages, tiny links, PDFs to download and fill by hand.
- Chatbot-only UIs with a wall of bubbles.
- SaaS dashboards, gradient heroes, dark neon.
- Official coats of arms or logos (we are a prototype, not the City).

## Strategic principles
1. One question, one screen.
2. Show the work: what Claude read, where each step comes from.
3. The person decides: she checks and signs, she confirms, she can stop the voice.
4. Honest states: errors and missing data are shown, never faked.
