# Milano Evolution

> Claude Impact Lab Milano · 3 October 2026 · Track 01

**One line:** a voice guide in your own language that gets to know you while you talk and gives international students and newcomers their first plan for Milano, with official sources.

**Demo video:** _coming before 16:00_

## The problem

Milano trains international talent and then loses it. People who come to study or just moved here face pages in Italian, steps in an order nobody explains, and a city that switches to English the moment they try Italian. They never feel part of Milano, and after graduation many leave.
